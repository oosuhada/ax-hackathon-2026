# Sample 4-Week PoC Plan

## PoC 1: Internal policy Q&A assistant

- Process: Internal policy Q&A
- Target department: HR
- Pain point: Employees repeatedly ask about benefits and HR policies
- Week 1: define workflow, collect sample documents/data, define success metrics
- Week 2: build prompt/skill prototype and human review workflow
- Week 3: test on sample cases and record failure modes
- Week 4: refine, document governance checklist, prepare executive demo
- Human-in-the-loop: required before external/client-facing use
