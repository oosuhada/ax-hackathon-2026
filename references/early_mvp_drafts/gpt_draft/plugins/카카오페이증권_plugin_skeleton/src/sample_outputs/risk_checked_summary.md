## 6. Beginner Clarity Improvements

- Separate what happened, why it may matter, and what could go wrong.
- Avoid one-directional phrases such as ‘상승 가능성이 높다’ unless uncertainty and downside risks are stated together.
- Define technical terms such as valuation, volatility, liquidity, and exchange-rate risk in plain language.
